Clause 1 — Data We Collect

When you register for a Smart Farmer account, we collect your name, your contact information (phone number), and your login credentials. Your password is stored only in securely hashed form and is never visible to anyone, including us.

You may optionally provide your region and your preferred crops to personalise the information you see. Location data is only ever collected with your explicit consent — the platform works fully without it.

Clause 2 — How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for exactly two purposes: to provide you with crop information and planting records, and to improve the Smart Farmer platform. Nothing else.

We never sell your data. We never share your data with third parties without your explicit consent.

Clause 3 — Your Right to Delete Your Account

You can delete your Smart Farmer account at any time from your account settings — no questions asked and no waiting period to request it.

When you delete your account, all of your personal data — your profile and your saved records — is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.

The crop information, educational content, and forecasts provided by Smart Farmer are for general guidance only. Growing conditions vary by soil, season, and location, and Smart Farmer does not guarantee that the information is accurate or suitable for every context.

Before making major farming decisions — such as changing crops, buying inputs, or altering planting schedules — please consult your local agricultural extension officer.
