Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Last updated July 2026

In short: we only collect what we need to give you crop information, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account at any time.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, phone number, and password. Your password is stored in a secure, encrypted form that no one can read, including us.

You can also add your region and preferred crops to personalise what you see. These are optional. We only collect your location if you give clear permission, and the app works fully without it.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to give you crop information and to improve the platform.

We never sell your data, and we never share it with anyone else unless you clearly agree.

3. Your Right to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from your account settings. There is no waiting period and no need to give a reason.

Once you delete your account, all your personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.

4. Accuracy Disclaimer

Our crop information is a general guide. Growing conditions change with soil, season, and place, so we cannot promise it fits every situation.

Before making big decisions like changing crops or buying supplies, talk to your local agricultural extension officer.

5. Informed Consent

By creating an account, you confirm that you are 18 or older, that you have read this policy, and that you agree to the data collection described in Clause 1.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account.
